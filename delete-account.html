Birthday Tracker

Delete your Birthday Tracker account

You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  • Open the app and go to Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email birthdaytracker.help@outlook.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Delete some data, keep your account

You can delete parts of your data in the app at any time, without deleting your account:

  • A birthday: open it, then Delete. It's also removed from your backup.
  • A wishlist item: open your wishlist, then delete the item.
  • A friend: in your friends list, tap ⋯ next to the friend, then Remove. You stop seeing each other's birthday and wishlist.
  • Your profile photo, name or birthday: Profile → Edit profile.
  • An AI gift chat: open the chat, then Start over. Chats are only saved on your phone.

You can also email birthdaytracker.help@outlook.com and tell us which data to delete. We'll do it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login
  • Your profile: name, @username, photo and birthday
  • Your birthday backup (the birthdays you added while signed in)
  • Your friends list and friend requests
  • Your wishlist and the gifts you reserved
  • Your invite records

AI usage records are deleted automatically after 2 days. Birthdays saved on your phone stay there: delete them in the app or uninstall the app. Crash reports don't contain your name or email and are deleted automatically within 90 days.

If you have a subscription, deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.
