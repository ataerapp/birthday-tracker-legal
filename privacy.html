Privacy Policy: Birthday Tracker

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Birthday Tracker ("the app", "we") helps you remember birthdays, connect with friends and share wishlists. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We keep it short and only collect what the app needs.

1. What stays only on your phone

If you use the app without an account, everything stays on your device:

  • Birthdays you add: name, date, optional photo, and the "About" details you enter (relationship, interests, style, budget, notes).
  • Your settings: theme and colours, which reminders you want, and birthday effects.
  • Your AI gift chats, so you can continue them later. You can delete a chat anytime with "Start over".

We never see this information. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no server is involved.

2. What we store when you create an account

If you sign in (with email, Google or Apple), we store on our servers:

  • Account: your email address and a secure login handled by our provider (we never see or store your password).
  • Profile: your name, @username, optional photo and birthday, so your friends can see it.
  • Friends: who you're friends with and pending friend requests.
  • Wishlist: the items you add (name, optional link and price), and gifts you secretly reserve on friends' wishlists.
  • Invites: if you joined with a friend's invite code, we record who invited you, so they can see their progress toward invite rewards (they see only your name, @username and photo).
  • AI usage count: when you use the AI gift assistant, we record the time of each request (not its content) to apply the daily limit. These records are deleted after 2 days.
  • Extra AI messages: if you buy extra messages, we count how many of them you've used.

Who can see it:

  • Only people you've accepted as friends can see your birthday and wishlist.
  • Searching by @username shows only your name, username and photo, never your birthday.
  • Wishlist owners can never see who reserved their items.

3. AI gift ideas

The AI gift assistant is included in the paid subscription. When you use it, the app sends the details you entered about that person (for example: relationship, interests, style, budget, notes, days until their birthday, and their unreserved wishlist items) and your chat messages to Google's Gemini AI to generate suggestions. We don't store your chats on our servers; they're saved only on your phone. Please don't type sensitive personal information into the chat.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: hosts our database, login and server functions.
  • Google (Gemini API): generates AI gift ideas.
  • Google / Apple: only if you choose "Continue with Google" or "Sign in with Apple".
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: handle payments. We never see your card details.
  • RevenueCat: tells the app whether your subscription is active and which extra message packs you bought. It receives a purchase ID linked to your account, never your card details.
  • Brevo: sends our account emails (like your sign-up confirmation and password reset codes). It receives your email address only to deliver them.
  • Sentry: receives crash reports when the app breaks, so we can fix it. A report contains the error, your phone model and system version, and which screen it happened on. It never includes your name, email, birthdays or anything you typed. Data is stored in the EU.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: a quick automatic check during sign-up and sign-in that you're a real person, not a bot.
  • PostHog: shows us how the app is used, so we can improve it. It receives which screens you open and which features you use (for example "added a birthday" or "started the free month"), your phone model, system and app version, and, once you sign in, your account ID. It never receives your name, email, birthdays, notes or chat messages. Your IP address is only used to estimate your country and isn't stored. Data is stored in the EU.

We do not sell your data, and we don't show ads.

5. Notifications

Birthday reminders are local notifications created on your phone. They never show the person's name. You can choose which reminders you get, or turn them off, in Profile → Settings → Reminders, or in your phone's settings.

6. Deleting your data

  • Account: Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, friends list, wishlist and invite records from our servers right away.
  • Data on your phone: delete birthdays in the app, or uninstall the app.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your login is stored in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android). Our database only lets each person read their own data and what friends have shared with them.

8. Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: birthdaytracker.help@outlook.com
